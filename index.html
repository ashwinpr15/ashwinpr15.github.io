Formula 1 Cost Cap & Competitive Balance

BA780 Introduction to Data Analytics · BU Questrom · Team B05 · 2026

54.72% → 52.14%Top-two points share, before vs after the cap
p = 0.245Welch test: inconclusive
69.79%Big Three share after the cap, excluding 2025

Approach

  • Downloads a fixed F1DB data release and checks its SHA-256 before use.
  • Audits missing values and duplicates, joins constructors, standings and race results, and documents team renames.
  • Computes each constructor's share of season points and compares 2010–2020 with 2021–2025, giving every season equal weight.

Limits

Five post-cap seasons is a small sample, and the 2022 aerodynamic rule changes overlap the cap, so its effect can't be isolated. Points share measures sporting results, not spending.

Headset Desk

C++ · Qt 6 / QML · CMake · v0.5.0-beta.1

What it does

  • Reads model, battery, charging state, firmware and codec from the headset over Bluetooth.
  • Noise Cancelling / Ambient / Off, ambient level and voice passthrough, five EQ bands and Clear Bass.
  • A Windows 11 style interface with a collapsible navigation pane, light/dark themes, a tray icon and keyboard focus rings.
  • Every change is sent once and confirmed by reading it back; nothing is silently resent.

Shipping

CI builds an installer and a portable ZIP with checksums. It installs per user without admin rights. It builds on protocol work from marconvcm/sony-device-center (MIT), with attribution in the repo.

Limits

Experimental beta: reads are verified on the listed devices; changing settings is opt-in and not yet verified across firmware versions.

Signal Denoising Autoencoder

TensorFlow / Keras · NumPy · Matplotlib

  • Generates 128 × 128 line patterns, adds Gaussian noise and trains a two-stage convolutional autoencoder (MSE loss).
  • Compares signal-to-noise ratio before and after reconstruction and saves comparison plots.

Limits

Inputs are synthetic matrices, not audio recordings, so this shows the denoising technique, not real audio quality.

Random Forest Benchmark

Python · scikit-learn · pandas

  • Generates five-feature records with a noisy rule-based label.
  • Compares a depth-limited 100-tree model with a 300-tree model on a stratified 80/20 split, reporting the signed change. It can go down as well as up.
  • Includes small tests for the reporting logic.

Limits

Fixed configurations on synthetic data. This is a learning exercise, not a diagnostic or clinical tool.

Network Traffic Classifier

TensorFlow / Keras · scikit-learn · joblib

  • Stratified 60/20/20 train/validation/test split of raw records.
  • Scaling and one-hot encoding are fitted on training data only; early stopping uses validation, and the test set is touched once.
  • Saves the model, the preprocessor and an evaluation record, with tests for unseen categories and serialization.

Limits

Records and labels are generated, so the scores show the pipeline working, not real-world intrusion detection.
