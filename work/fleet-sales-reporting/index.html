Automating Stellantis' fleet sales report

At Tata Consultancy Services, I automated Stellantis' fleet sales report end to end, from data extraction to the final report, replacing a manual process.

Estimated ~$80K (₹70 lakh) a year in manual reporting cost saved.

An estimate of the annual cost of the manual work the automation replaced. The dollar figure converts ₹70 lakh at about ₹87.5 per US dollar.

Business problem

Before the automation, producing Stellantis' fleet sales report took manual work on every cycle, from pulling the data to assembling the final report.

My role

I built the automation as a Business Intelligence Developer on the TCS team supporting Stellantis.

Approach

The automation covers the whole path from data extraction to the finished report, so no step depends on someone running it by hand.

Result

  • The report runs end to end without manual assembly.
  • Estimated savings of about ₹70 lakh (~$80K) a year in manual work.

Limitations

The report, data and code belong to the client and aren't public. The diagram shows the shape of the pipeline with generic labels and no client data. The savings figure is an estimate, not an audited number.

Artifacts

Client work isn't public. The project is summarized on my resume.
