Headset Desk

A Windows 11 app for Sony WH-1000XM5 and WH-CH720N headphones: battery, noise control, a five-band EQ and a tray icon.

Stack
C++, Qt 6 / QML, CMake
Release
v0.5.0-beta.1
Shipping
Installer and portable ZIP
Status
Experimental beta

What it does

  • Reads model, battery, charging state, firmware and codec from the headset over Bluetooth.
  • Noise Cancelling / Ambient / Off, ambient level and voice passthrough, five EQ bands and Clear Bass.
  • A Windows 11 style interface with a collapsible navigation pane, light and dark themes, a tray icon and keyboard focus rings.
  • Every change is sent once and confirmed by reading it back; nothing is silently resent.

Shipping

CI builds an installer and a portable ZIP with checksums. It installs per user without admin rights. It builds on protocol work from marconvcm/sony-device-center (MIT), with attribution in the repository.

Limitations

Experimental beta: reads are verified on the listed devices; changing settings is opt-in and not yet verified across firmware versions.
