Migrating QlikView applications to Power BI

At Tata Consultancy Services, I migrated 6+ business applications from QlikView to Power BI, rebuilding their data models and reports.

Role
Business Intelligence Developer
Employer
Tata Consultancy Services
Scope
6+ applications
Tools
QlikView, Power BI

6+ QlikView applications rebuilt in Power BI.

Each migration rebuilt the application's data model and reports in Power BI.

Business problem

These business applications ran on QlikView and were being moved to Power BI, which meant rebuilding each one's data model and reports rather than copying them across.

My role

I migrated 6+ of these applications as a Business Intelligence Developer at TCS.

Approach

A migration is a rebuild, not a restyle: each part of a QlikView application has a different counterpart in Power BI.

Result

6+ applications moved from QlikView to Power BI with rebuilt data models and reports.

Limitations

The applications and data belong to the client and aren't public. The diagram describes the general mapping between the two tools, not a specific client application.
